# Impact Statement

## Project: Agro-Resilience Agent

### The Problem
Climate change is causing unpredictable weather patterns, leading to crop failure for small-scale farmers in India who lack access to timely expert advice.

### The Solution
An AI-powered chatbot that democratizes access to agronomy expertise. It combines:
1.  **IBM Granite AI:** For reasoning and advice.
2.  **OpenWeatherMap:** For real-time environmental context.
3.  **Local Knowledge:** RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) on Indian crop guides.

### Expected Impact
- **Economic:** Reduced crop loss through early pest warnings.
- **Environmental:** Optimized water usage suggestions (SDG 6).
- **Social:** Bridging the digital divide with multilingual support (Hindi/English).
